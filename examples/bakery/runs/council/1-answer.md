# Council verdict: launch with pay-at-pickup, not online payments (5 of 5 against)

I ran the question as: should Lucia recommend that Kvások & syn launch the new pre-order system **with online card payments from day one**? All five members voted against, in both rounds, with no vote changing sides.

| Member | Round 1 | Round 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 optimist | NO 72 | NO 76 |
| 🔴 skeptic | NO 75 | NO 70 |
| 🛠️ engineer | NO 78 | NO 80 |
| 💰 business | NO 72 | NO 74 |
| 👤 client (Jana) | NO 70 | NO 78 |

**Top objection (skeptic, round 2):** everyone in Round 1 said big cakes "already carry a 30 % deposit". But that deposit is taken in the shop (`kvasok-a-syn.md:18`). Once cakes can be ordered online, that protection is lost right before Christmas. So "add payments later" isn't enough on its own.

## Moderator's verdict
**REJECTED.** YES 0/5 (0 %), weighted 0 %, against a 60 % threshold.

**Recommendation:**
- Launch with pay-at-pickup.
- Quote online cake deposits as a **separate optional line**: about 8–10 h, 400–500 € excl. VAT, with refunds done by hand in the Stripe dashboard.
- Confirm that line only after Jana's texts arrive and her accountant has cleared how eKasa (Slovakia's electronic sales-recording system) treats online payments.
- Until then, cakes over 50 € still need their deposit: either in the shop as today, or through a payment link that staff send after the order.

**Why:**
- **Budget:** the base price is about 3 400 €. Adding the 700–900 € payments add-on gives 4 100–4 300 € excl. VAT, about 40 % over Jana's "around 3 000 €". If her 3 000 € includes VAT, even the base price is about 36 % over.
- **Small gain, tight schedule:** no-shows cost about 93 €/week gross (about 59 € on bread, 34 € on cakes). There are about 4 weeks to early December, and Lucia can't promise any date before texts and photos arrive.

**Open disagreements:**
- **Payment link from day one?** Engineer, optimist and Jana want it. Business says staff would have to match payments to orders by hand during the rush, which is exactly the staff burden Jana fears. Skeptic would rather make big cakes phone-only.
- **How big is the December cake loss?** Business says about 34 €/week; Jana thinks that's low for Christmas. Nobody has last December's figures.
- **Cost of deposits only:** 8–10 h assumes refunds stay manual. The price list implies 14–18 h with a proper refund flow.

**Evidence corrections:**
- **English:** in Round 2 Jana said she "never asked for" English. Her notes say it "would be nice". It stays as an optional line (300–450 €).
- **Texts by 15 November:** this was the simulated Jana speaking, not something the real Jana said. No date can rest on it.
- **The florist project's 5→6-week slip** was cited as a payments risk, but that project had no payments. The week was lost to late texts, and the extra hours went into slot rules.
- **"The café with Stripe took 8 weeks"** doesn't isolate payments either. That project had 3 locations and two languages.
- **Stripe fee:** 1.5 % + 0.25 € works out to about 0.46 € on a 14 € order, not the 0.30 € Jana estimated. A newer Stripe notice shows 1.4 %, so check the dashboard.
- **eKasa law:** Act 384/2025 exists and has been in force since 1.1.2026. How it treats payments through an online gateway is **unverified**; Jana's accountant has to answer that.

**Before quoting, ask Jana:**
1. Is the 3 000 € including or excluding VAT?
2. On what date will texts and photos arrive? Please confirm in writing.
3. Last December, how many cakes were never picked up, split into over and under 50 €?
4. Can she ask her accountant whether online deposits have to be recorded in eKasa, and how?
5. Does she want English at launch?
6. Until online deposits exist, is it OK that big cakes are confirmed only once the deposit is paid?

The council only advises; whether and what to send Jana is your call.
