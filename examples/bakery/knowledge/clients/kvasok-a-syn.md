# Kvások & syn (bakery)

Updated: 2026-11-02 · Purpose: quote · Source: discovery call with the owner, 2026-11-02 (Lucia's notes)

> Fictional bakery and owner, invented for the claude-crews example. All numbers are made up.

## Snapshot

- Family bakery in Bratislava–Ružinov, one shop, 7 employees. Owner: Jana Kováčová.
- Sourdough breads, pastries, and celebration cakes made to order.
- Open Tue–Sat 7:00–18:00, Sun 7:00–12:00, closed Mon.
- Has a one-page website (Slovak + English, contact form to email), built in 2024 by Lucia. Repo: `~/code/kvasok-site`.

## How they take orders today

- About 70 pre-orders a week by phone and Instagram messages (bread and pastry boxes, average 14 €), plus about 15 cakes a week (average 38 €).
- Phone rings during the 7:00–9:00 rush; Jana estimates 5–10 missed calls a day. Instagram messages sometimes go unanswered until evening.
- No-shows: about 6 % of pre-orders are never picked up. For cakes it hurts most; for cakes over 50 € they already take a 30 % deposit in the shop.
- Card terminal at the counter (bank terminal). No online payments today.

## What Jana asked for

- "Customers should order online and pick a time to collect."
- Wants it before the Christmas orders start, early December.
- Budget: "around 3 000 €, a bit more if it clearly pays off".
- Worried about: payment fees, refunds when a cake goes wrong, and staff having to learn a new system.
- Sunday market brings tourists; English "would be nice".
